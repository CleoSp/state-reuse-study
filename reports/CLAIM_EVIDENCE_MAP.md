# Claim-to-evidence map for `reports/paper/MANUSCRIPT.md`

Every quantitative sentence in the manuscript traces to a row here. "Records" names the sealed job directories under `runs/confirmatory_v1` (protocol commit `cdaf843`, protocol SHA-256 `ea3020f7…237a1`, matrix SHA-256 `7c71eb25…0eeba`, config `configs/confirmatory_tier2_frozen.json`). "Regenerate" names the script and the section of `reports/confirmatory/RESULTS.md` (written by `scripts/synthesize_confirmatory.py`; per-input record hashes in `reports/confirmatory/manifest.json`) or the audit script. Seeds are the five independent backbone/adapter seeds 29, 43, 71, 101, 137 unless stated. Roots are the 256 ordinary test roots per suite. Intervals are paired-root 95% bootstrap intervals (10,000 draws, seed 64037, seeds averaged within roots); "crossed" also resamples seeds. Status: **C** = confirmatory (frozen before test generation), **E** = exploratory (prespecified as secondary or not prespecified; labeled in the manuscript), **D** = development/validation evidence (pilot; no test roots), **A** = independent audit reproduction. Synthetic fixtures and privileged diagnostics never enter principal comparisons; the one privileged row is marked.
